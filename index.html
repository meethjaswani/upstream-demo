How it works

  1. The map. 12 companies and 26 deals, taken from filings and press releases. Each arrow carries its amount, date, source and a confidence tag.
  2. The scenario. Your text becomes a company, a size and a type. With a local API key Claude reads it. Without one, a keyword parser does.
  3. The shock. If a buyer cuts spending, each seller loses that share of what the buyer pays it per year. The math runs in your browser.
  4. Next hops. Each hit company cuts its own purchases in proportion to its revenue hit, damped by 0.6 per hop, for up to 4 hops. This damping is our assumption.
  5. Stakes. Shown separately from revenue: stake value times valuation drop, where the drop is assumed to be 1.5 times the shock.
  6. Money loops. A cycle search over the supply and investment graph.
  7. Choke points. Each company is failed in full, as a buyer and as a supplier, and ranked by revenue at risk times companies hit. The top three carry a diamond.

The AI steps shown in a report are a recorded run. Not investment advice.
